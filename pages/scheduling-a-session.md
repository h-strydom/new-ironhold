**Sessions are booked by players.** Not by the DM, and not on a fixed night of the week.

## How it actually works

1. **Find people.** Four to six at the table, though three will run. Rounding up a group is yours to do, not the DM's.
1. **Decide what you are doing.** Either claim an open job from the quest board, or say what you want to go and do: explore in a direction, chase something specific, general adventuring.
1. **Agree a time** that suits the people you found.
1. **Tell the DM.** Who, what, when. Only then does a session exist.

## What that means in practice

- **Any night will do.** If the people you found want a Tuesday, play on a Tuesday.
- **Play as much or as little as you want.** Every week, once a month, twice in one weekend. There is no minimum.
- **Sessions run three hours at most.** That is a ceiling, not a target. When the time is up the session ends, finished or not, and the job carries over to the next one.

## Group size

Four to six players is a full table. Three will run. Below three it is usually worth waiting or recruiting. More than six splits into two sessions.
