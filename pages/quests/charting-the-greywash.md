> SURVEY DIRECTIVE: THE GREYWASH
>
> By order of [Pioneers](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/r0szif5c): the Greywash remains uncharted past the first bend west of [New Ironhold](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/x8tqb3v2). Two earlier surveys turned back. One lost a canoe to something nesting in the reeds upstream. One reached a narrowing where the river runs to rapids nobody has mapped a safe line through.
>
> Chart the river upstream as far as it can safely be travelled. Identify what took the canoe. Neutralise it, or confirm a route around it.
>
> Report findings to Guildmaster [Corin Bregard](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/cwpwkb2krxdd3nk8rpafsll6) on completion. Partial charts accepted where the full route cannot be run.
