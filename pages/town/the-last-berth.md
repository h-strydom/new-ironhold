[[New Ironhold|New Ironhold's]] inn, and the only building in town that everybody passes. Run by [[Dorne Aldwick]] and [[Liora Aldwick]].

It stands at the landward end of the dock on the east side, so every arrival, every departure and every crate off every ship goes past its windows. That is most of why [[Liora Aldwick|Liora]] knows what she knows without leaving the building.

The plot came with a condition written into it: adventurers are housed free upstairs, one room each, for as long as there are adventurers to house.

## Food and drink

The water ration and the expedition meal cost nothing and taste like it. Everything else costs, and The Last Berth is the only place inside the palisade serving anything a person would choose to eat.
