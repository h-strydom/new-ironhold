[[New Ironhold|New Ironhold's]] inn, and the only building in town that everybody passes. Run by [Dorne Aldwick](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/s7bsp8yt) and [Liora Aldwick](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/j9ek6yut).

It stands at the landward end of the dock on the west side, so every arrival, every departure and every crate off every ship goes past its windows. That is most of why Liora knows what she knows without leaving the building.

## The rooms

The plot came with a condition written into it: adventurers are housed free upstairs, one room each, for as long as there are adventurers to house.

No adventurer has yet claimed one. Twelve expeditions have landed, and the rooms stand made up and empty.

## Food and drink

The water ration and the expedition meal cost nothing and taste like it. Everything else costs, and the Last Berth is the only place inside the palisade serving anything a person would choose to eat.
