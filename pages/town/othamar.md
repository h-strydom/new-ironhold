[Othamar](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/hkrwfmbz)'s yard sells weapons and tools, limited to what its engineers have finished. [Garrick Thane](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/fb2zw9rw) is usually found there and haggles over nothing but the work.

Every weapon here carries a permanent **-1 to attack rolls**. The smith in [New Ironhold](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/x8tqb3v2) has stood idle since the mine was infested, and there is no metal stock to forge a proper edge or mend a bent one. What the yard sells came off the last ship or was scavenged and reworked.
