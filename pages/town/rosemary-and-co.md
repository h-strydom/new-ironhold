The general store, just down the hill from the Pioneer's tent. Run by [[Rosemary]].

Day-to-day goods and nothing else: rope, lamp oil, boots, canvas. Weapons and tools are [[id:01M854GJFBAES917146Q2016YD|Othamar's]] trade, and rations and potions are [Threnval](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/dxejh6gh)'s.

She gives away whatever came out of the oven that day. A biscuit, still warm, on a customer's first visit and most visits after, whether or not they buy. She will not take payment for one.

## The building

The shop goes up rather than back. Shelving runs to the roof beam, and threaded through it is a lattice of ramps, walkways and rope-railed gantries small enough to sit in cupped hands. Every shelf in the place can be reached on foot by somebody five inches tall who cannot fly.

## Terms

No credit, for anyone: not adventurers, not the companies, not the [Pioneers](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/r0szif5c). It is the only fixed rule in the shop.
