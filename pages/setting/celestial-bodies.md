Three moons hang over the world, and everyone here calls them the Three Sisters. Nearly everything about how time is measured traces back to them: the length of a month, the days ships are willing to sail, the nights honest folk bolt their doors.

They are not equals, and nobody pretends otherwise. [[Solenne]] is the eldest and the only one anyone truly relies on. [[Valora]] is the middle sister, and she keeps her own counsel. [[Umbriel]] is the youngest, and most people would rather not discuss her at all.

## The sisters

| Sister | Also called | Full moon |
| - | - | - |
| [[Solenne]] | The Sovereign, the First Sister | The 1st, every month, without fail |
| [[Valora]] | The Crimson Sister, the Blood Moon | Three or four times a year, never on schedule |
| [[Umbriel]] | The Dark Sister, the Veil | The 33rd, every month |

Solenne is large, bright and perfectly circular, and her cycle *is* the month: thirty-five days, no drift, full on the first. Valora is mid-sized and rust-red with an unsteady aura, and her orbit wanders far enough that astrologers sell windows rather than dates. Umbriel is small and livid, the colour of a bruise a few days into healing, hardly worth looking for on most nights, and reliably visible only when full.

## Reading the sky

Any settlement of size keeps an almanac, and any almanac worth its vellum prints the same three columns. Solenne's dates are the calendar itself, the days on which work is done. Umbriel's are the nights it is not, and they are set in heavier ink. Valora gets no dates at all, only a Crimson Window, which is a guess with a reputation attached: mainland astrologers stake careers on narrowing it, and the good ones get within three days.

No almanac charts Umbriel's phases between one full moon and the next. Nobody has ever managed it, and the ones who tried hardest are not consulted.

## What the sisters bring

Solenne's full night is [Solenne](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/xwcy6c08), the one night of the month nobody dreads. Umbriel's is [Umbriel](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/am4xsyhf), and the doors stay barred from dusk. Valora keeps no night anyone can plan for, except one: once a year she goes full beside Umbriel, and that night is the [Sister's Scourge](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/myd2z1bv).

## The shape of a month

A month opens on the Bright Shift and all but ends on the Veil. The heavy work and the shipping fall in the first five days, under [Solenne](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/xwcy6c08). The 33rd is shut, and the two days after it are for putting right whatever the shutting interrupted. Then it starts again.

## The shape of a year

Eleven months make a year. The eleventh does not end the way the others do: its 33rd carries the [Sister's Scourge](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/myd2z1bv), and the year turns two days later.
