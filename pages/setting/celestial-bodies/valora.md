The Crimson Sister. The Blood Moon. Middle of the three in age and in size, deep rust-red, wrapped in an aura that never sits quite still.

Valora goes full three or four times a year, and no one can name the night in advance. Her orbit runs to a little over a hundred days, but she comes early, then late, then early again, and the drift accumulates. Apart from one night a year, she does not return to the same date twice.

Since nobody can give a date, astrologers give a window instead: a span of days inside which Valora is expected to go full. A poor astrologer offers a fortnight. A good one offers three days. Mainland crowns and great houses keep the good ones on retainer, and a Crimson Window is one of the few forecasts anyone will move a wedding or a shipment for. Farmers treat the whole window as the event and get their business done before it opens.

When Valora reaches full phase her glare stains the clouds red and the horizon goes the colour of a banked fire. Beasts that go docile under [Solenne](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/xwcy6c08) turn aggressive under Valora, and that is the danger of her: everyone grows up expecting a full moon to calm them. Tides surge past their marks. Tempers run short, and spells misbehave in hands that are usually steady.

The mainland treats her full night as one to prepare for rather than to fear. War, passion and dangerous work all belong to Valora, and mainland chronicles are thick with battles fixed to her windows.

## The night she is not alone

Once a year she goes full on the same night as [Umbriel](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/am4xsyhf), the youngest of the three: the [Sister's Scourge](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/myd2z1bv).
