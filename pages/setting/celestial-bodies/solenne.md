The Sovereign. The First Sister. Eldest of the three, and the steadiest thing in the sky.

Solenne is a silver disc, large, bright and perfectly round. Her cycle is thirty-five days and it does not drift by so much as an hour. Her full moon falls on the first day of every month, and always has. The month is not a span of time that happens to suit her. The month is her.

Everything else is measured against her because nothing else can be. Sailors set courses by her. Harvests are timed to her. Where her sisters are read as temperament, Solenne is read as law.

## The Bright Shift

Her full night falls on the 1st of every month, and it is the one night with formal customs attached to it in every mainland nation that kept any customs at all. Formally it is a day of order. In practice it is the longest working day of the month, and the only one nobody dreads.

Work starts before first light and runs well past dark, and nobody tires. People come in off the fields as if from an easy afternoon. Whole villages turn out for the work that needs more hands than one household has: raising, hauling, roofing, digging out. A party on the road under her spends nothing of its stores to the day and does not lose its way.

Old mainland law punished violence on a Bright Shift as a separate and heavier offence. Most of that law has lapsed into custom, and the custom is well kept: quarrels wait for the second. The colony brought the custom with it, and it is the nearest thing to a holiday anyone on [[New Ironhold]] gets.

Under a full Solenne the night runs about as bright as late twilight, bright enough to read by outdoors and to work by. Towns of any size hold night markets, and at the tail of the season the harvest runs on into the dark without anyone lighting a torch. Predatory night-beasts go docile under her light. Nobody has explained it, and everybody plans around it. That makes the Bright Shift the safest night of the month to be outside a wall, which matters a great deal more here than it ever did on the mainland.

## The crossing

Ships cross to and from [[New Ironhold]] only in the first five days of each month, opening on the Bright Shift. Everything that arrives, arrives then: mail, supplies, replacement expedition members, and anything ordered from home half a year ago. Everything that leaves, leaves then as well. Those five days are the busiest of the month in town and the emptiest in the field.

## Her sisters

[Valora](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/nsr5t74l) is the middle sister and [[Umbriel]] the youngest. Solenne is the only one of the three whose behaviour is settled.
