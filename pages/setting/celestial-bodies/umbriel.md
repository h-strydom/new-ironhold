The Dark Sister. The Veil. Youngest of the three, and the one people would rather not bring up.

Umbriel is small and livid, purple at the centre and going yellow at the rim, like a bruise a few days into healing. Her cycle is thirty-five days, the same as Solenne's, and her full moon falls on the 33rd of every month, three days before the next one opens. Nothing else about the two of them is alike.

## The phases nobody can chart

Umbriel is reliably visible only when full, as a dull ring low in the sky. Between one full moon and the next she is a matter of opinion. Two people standing in the same field on the same night will each describe something, and the descriptions will not match. Neither of them is lying.

Astrologers have tried to chart her intermediate phases for centuries. Several have gone mad at it, and the profession counts that a hazard of the work rather than a warning. There is no accepted chart, and no accepted method for producing one.

## The Veil

Her full night is called the Veil, and her full moon is bad luck, plainly and without much theological hedging. Crops fail more often after it than before. People see things that are not there, and now and then things that are. The old phrasing is that the veil between the living and the rest goes thin.

Ward the thresholds. Bar the doors. Bring the livestock in. Do not travel, do not open to a knock, and do not go looking for whoever was knocking.

The rituals vary by region and are mostly cheap: salt, chalk, a blessed stone at the door, a particular knot tied in a particular way. Nobody claims they work reliably. Everybody performs them.

Nothing is scheduled on [New Ironhold](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/x8tqb3v2) for the 33rd. No caravan leaves, no survey goes out, and the gates stay shut from dusk. A party caught outside the wall makes camp, barricades it, and goes nowhere until morning, and that day of the road is simply lost. The settlement kept the custom from the mainland without argument.

## The night she is not alone

Once a year [Valora](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/nsr5t74l) is full on the same night: the [Sister's Scourge](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/myd2z1bv).
