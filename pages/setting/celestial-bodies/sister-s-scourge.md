Once a year, on Undecima 33rd, [[Umbriel]] and [Valora](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/nsr5t74l) reach full phase on the same night, and the year turns two days later. Its other name is the Bloody Veil.

Umbriel is full on the 33rd of every month, so her half of the night is fixed. Valora's half is not, and her arrival on that exact night is either the great coincidence of the calendar or evidence that the calendar was arranged around it. Astrologers have argued both readings and settled neither.

## What the night brings

Beasts that turn aggressive under Valora alone turn savage under both sisters. The dead do not always stay buried. Arcane work turns dangerous: spells surge or misfire, and fissures open in the wilds and in town streets alike. It is the reverse of [Solenne](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/xwcy6c08)'s night, when beasts go docile and nobody tires.

Towns go silent. Torches are doused and watches doubled, and everyone waits behind a barricade without a sound until it passes. The mainland calls this the Unspoken Rule.

## Preparation Week

Preparation Week is the sixth week of Undecima, the 26th to the 30th. Windows are boarded, livestock hauled in, food stockpiled and threshold stones blessed. Trade halts across the mainland and does not resume until the new year. Nothing else in the year comes as close to being a season of its own.
