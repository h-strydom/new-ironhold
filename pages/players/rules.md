## Ask

If you want to do something, ask. Chase a rumour, go where nobody has been, take up a trade, hunt one specific thing, build something.

Be specific, and ask early. "We go exploring" gets you a session built out of whatever is to hand. "We want to find where the Greywash comes from" gets you one built around that.

## Party size

Four to six players per expedition, flexible either way. Roll as many characters as you like. Only one of them goes out in any given session.

## What you keep track of

Maps, dates, names, directions, and who told you what. All yours. Nobody will draw the map for you, keep the calendar for you, or remember on your behalf.

Your character knows what they would know. Ask what they can see, what they recognise, what they have heard, and you get a straight answer. Spend a week past the wall and come back, and nobody will tell you the date.

A party that keeps good notes will find things a careless one walks straight past.

## [Quests](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/nj5mzdvg)

Work somebody is willing to pay for, posted at the Pioneer's tent. You are never required to take it. Taking work from the Pioneer or from either trading company does not bind you to whoever posted it, and you can move between them from one session to the next.

## If your character dies

- A new character starts a step below the party's current highest level.
- They arrive with the next monthly ship, which lands in the first five days of the month.
- They are equipped as the first wave was: free gear, rations, the [Currency](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/jmc0vj0n) stipend and a 150 GP-equivalent equipment budget.
