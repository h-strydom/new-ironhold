## Do I get my class's normal starting equipment?

No.

You get a winter clothing set, a summer clothing set, basic camping gear, one month of rations, and **150 GP-equivalent** to spend on whatever you like from the equipment chapter, as [[Character Creation]] sets out.

Three reasons for doing it this way:

**It simplifies character creation.** One budget and one shopping trip, instead of cross-referencing a class package against the things you actually wanted.

**It puts everyone on equal footing.** No class starts richer than another by accident of its equipment list. 150 GP sits at the top of the range the 2024 rules already use for alternate starting gold (50 to 155 GP, depending on class), so nobody starts far from their book-normal wealth either.

**It scales.** As the campaign continues, new characters start at higher levels and the budget rises with them. Under the normal rules a character joining at level 6 would arrive carrying a package designed for level 1; here they arrive with money that matches where the table actually is.
