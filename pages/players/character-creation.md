[[New Ironhold]] runs on Dungeons & Dragons 5.5e, the 2024 ruleset. No restriction on sourcebooks, species or classes: if it is in the 2024 rules, it is on the table.

## Level

Start at level 3.

Characters made later in the campaign start higher, always a step below the party's current highest level.

## Ability scores

Any method legal under the 2024 rules:

- Point buy
- The standard array
- Roll your own: 4d6, drop the lowest, six times
- A pre-rolled array of 16, 13, 13, 11, 10 and 8, assigned across your abilities however you like, for anyone who would rather skip the arithmetic

**Anything you roll yourself needs a witness.** Ability scores and hit points both: another player watches before the number is written down.

## Starting equipment

You do **not** get your class's normal starting equipment, for reasons the [FAQ](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/tdpw2ikx) sets out.

Free, and not counted against your budget:

- A winter clothing set
- A summer clothing set
- Basic camping gear: bedroll, tinderbox, rope, waterskin, mess kit

On top of that you have **150 GP-equivalent**, what your character earned adventuring alone before signing the expedition contract. Spend it on anything in the equipment chapter. If you want a heavy crossbow that no class list includes, buy one.

## Landing stipend

On arrival, before your first quest, every character also receives one month of rations and [[Currency|Gilda]].

## Submit your character

Before you play, send the DMs a PDF: the character sheet, a backstory, a physical description, and the equipment you're bringing on the expedition.
