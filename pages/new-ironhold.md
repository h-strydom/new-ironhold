The crossing took eleven weeks. You remember the storm that nearly took the main-mast, the week the water ration was cut twice, and the morning someone on watch swore they saw lights under the waves that weren't fish. None of that matters now. You're standing on a dock twelve winters old, in a town that has held since the first expedition made the crossing. You're the thirteenth. New Ironhold is what the twelve before you left behind.

A palisade, a harbour, and a handful of streets that turn to mud when it rains. Beyond the wall: everything else.

You signed the expedition contract for what's out there, not what's in here. New Ironhold is only the beginning. Which roads get cut, which ruins get mapped, which trade routes hold: expeditions like yours decide what it becomes.

A ship lands once a month, having sailed in [Solenne](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/xwcy6c08)'s week eleven weeks before, and most of what it puts on the dock is labour. Dozens of hands signed on for stone, timber, and whatever needs carrying up from the harbour. They go to the bunkhouse, all of them, one long room with the beds close enough together that every man hears his neighbour turn over in the night.

Adventurers do not arrive on that schedule. An expedition lands once a year, and a handful of adventurers come off it. This year that is you.

Nobody will ask you to haul stone, cut timber or empty a hold. That is not what you are for. You are for the work nobody signs on for: keeping a caravan alive between here and wherever it is going, clearing out whatever has moved into ground the town needs, and walking past the tree line to find out what is on the other side of it.

For that you get a room at the Last Berth. One each, to yourself, and you do not pay for it. That is where the privilege stops. The water ration and the expedition meal are free and taste like it, and anything you would actually choose to eat is bought the same way anybody else buys it.

Twelve expeditions landed before yours. None of the adventurers from those twelve are still working. **Whatever privilege you hold inside the wall, you pay for outside it, and the rate is not negotiable.**

Three bodies run New Ironhold, and between them they hand out most of the work worth doing.

The Pioneer settles disputes here. It chartered the whole venture, answers to the Continental Assembly, and calls the official surveys.

Trading companies followed The Pioneer out here chasing their own fortunes, private outfits with no obligation to anyone but their backers. Two operate out of New Ironhold at present: Othamar, hauling in engineers and machinery, and Threnval, dealing in reagents and old-world magecraft. Friendly rivals, not enemies, but both are competing for the same hands.

Post a job, take a job. Whoever's paying, the work is the same: go out there, come back with something worth having.

Gold means nothing here. What you're paid in is [Currenc](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/jmc0vj0n)y, marks the expedition issues against completed work, good for gear, information, and favours in town. What you find out there is worth having in its own right: a blade is still a blade, a cure is still a cure.

Ships sail only in [Solenne](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/xwcy6c08)'s week, when the Silver Sister's light keeps the seas calm enough to cross. Anything you need from the mainland that the new continent can't yet provide, you send for on the ship out. That includes you, if it comes to that.

## Before you go

Read up on [Character Creation](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/wwbmxrj1) before your first session. The rest, New Ironhold will show you.

**The wilderness doesn't care how the crossing went, or how far you came to get here. Not everyone who walks out past the wall walks back.**
