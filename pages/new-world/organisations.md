[[id:01M854GJFBAES917146Q2016YD|Othamar]] and [Threnval](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/dxejh6gh) compete for one thing: the labour of whoever takes work here. Neither wants the other gone, and neither has any use for a feud.

A stretch spent on one company's work is a stretch not spent on the other's, and the gap shows later, on the day someone needs what was never made. A company turns cold through neglect.

Nothing binds anyone to one employer, and anyone may take work from all three off the [Quests](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/nj5mzdvg) board.
