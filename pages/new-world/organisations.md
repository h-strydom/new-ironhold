[[id:01M854GJFBAES917146Q2016YD|Othamar]] and [[id:01M854JEW07BXK36ETZRPMBTCF|Threnval]] compete for one thing: the labour of whoever takes work here. Neither wants the other gone, and neither has any use for a feud.

A stretch spent on one company's work is a stretch not spent on the other's. Nothing binds anyone to one employer, and anyone may take work from all three off the [[Quests]] board.
