A trading company built on old-world engineers and machinists. Othamar wants raw material, machinery and salvage: anything they can study and turn into something that works.

Work that clears the way for building. Hauling ore, levelling ground, recovering machinery or components intact. Othamar pays less for the same object in pieces.

They are the fastest route to practical gear in [[New Ironhold]]: tools and weapons their engineers have finished.

Practical, hard-driving, unsentimental. Othamar has no use for glory or old-world magecraft. Where a clever rig will do the work of a spell, they would rather pay for the rig.

## Who speaks for them

[[Garrick Thane]], usually found in Othamar's yard.
