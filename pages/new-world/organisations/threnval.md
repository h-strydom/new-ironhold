A trading company built on old-world magecraft and alchemy. Its trade runs almost entirely on reagents out of Ashvale. Threnval wants rare herbs, monster components and anything else the wilderness yields that an alchemist can turn into something to sell.

##

Work that clears the way for gathering. Driving off whatever is fouling a good stand of herbs, recovering reagents from a kill instead of hacking it apart, escorting a forager somewhere too dangerous to reach alone.

They also hold the town's stock of potions, tonics and rations, shipped across from the old world at a price that carries everything that broke on the way.

## What they are like

Long-established, patient, exact. A spoiled reagent is worth nothing. They wait for a clean harvest and let the quick one go to someone else.
