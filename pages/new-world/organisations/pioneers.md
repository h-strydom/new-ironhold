The Pioneers chartered the expedition that made [[New Ironhold]] possible and funded the first survey ships. They answer to [[The Continental Assembly]] as a whole and to none of its seven countries alone. They are the only part of the assembly that reaches [[New Ironhold]], and the nearest thing it has to a government.

Their official work is surveys, mapping, securing routes and anything that adds to what [[New Ironhold]] knows of the continent under it.

They are the one employer that counts a journey that comes back with nothing but a map as done.

## Who speaks for them

[[Corin Bregard]], from the Pioneers' tent. He gives the trading companies' notices the same fair hearing as the Pioneers' own.
