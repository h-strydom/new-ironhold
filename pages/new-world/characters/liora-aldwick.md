Liora Aldwick runs [[The Last Berth]] with [[Dorne Aldwick]] and does the talking for both of them. Most of what [[New Ironhold]] knows about itself passes through her.

- **Wood elf**, five foot six, lithe.
- **Copper-red hair**, thick, coming loose from whatever was meant to hold it up.
- **Green eyes**, and freckles across the nose and shoulders.
- **Old burn marks** along the fingers, from carrying plates too hot for too long.

She has an opinion on everything and gives it before anybody asks. Her hands move as fast as her mouth, and she is never where she was last seen. The room she works never entirely empties, and she has not needed to raise her voice in it for years. She remembers every regular's usual order, and every regular's usual lie.

She wants to hear everything in town before anybody else does, and she does not sell what she knows. She trades it, and the only currency she accepts is more of it. The town's gossip runs through The Last Berth, not through the notice boards, and anybody who wants to know what they are walking into before taking on work asks her.

She married him on the mainland and crossed with him on an early expedition. The rooms upstairs come free by a condition on the plot, one to each adventurer, and she hands out the keys. Anything past the bed and the ration is priced.
