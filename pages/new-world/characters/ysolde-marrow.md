Ysolde Marrow speaks for [[id:01M854JEW07BXK36ETZRPMBTCF|Threnval]] in [New Ironhold](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/x8tqb3v2), and is precise in a way that reads as expensive.

- **Elf**, a shade under six foot, thin and straight-backed.
- **Ash-grey skin** drawn tight over the bones of her face.
- **Colourless hair** pulled back hard.
- **Eyes with no white in them** anywhere.
- **Black clothes**, high-collared, clean and unworn, in a town where everything else shows wear.
- **A case of glass vials** always within reach of her hand. She has never let anyone else carry it.

She holds still in a way that makes other people fidget, and has never given any sign of noticing. She opens with "You have something, or you want something," level and unhurried, and not shaped like a question. People slow down to match her without knowing they have.

She would rather wait a season for a clean harvest than pay for a rushed one at a discount, and she tells whoever is trying to hurry her exactly that. She is not cold. She is entirely comfortable telling a seller when she thinks a bad job is what she is being offered. She pays over the board rate for anything intact that came out of the ground here, and if it is already broken she will still look.

She was a senior alchemist before the Treaty of [Ashvale](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/s07t97v0) was signed. That puts her past a century at the least, and she will not be drawn on her age. [Threnval](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/dxejh6gh) sent her out with the second wave of investment. She was not the first company representative to arrive, but she is the first one who stayed.

:::secret
Her colouring is not fey blood. She was born beside [Ashvale](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/s07t97v0), and the forest changed her. Around [Ashvale](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/s07t97v0) what it does is called [Umbriel](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/am4xsyhf)'s kiss, and she was not expected to live out her first week. Many of the children born near her that season did not.
:::

:::secret
[Threnval](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/dxejh6gh) sent her to learn whether this continent can replace [Ashvale](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/s07t97v0), and she has told nobody in [New Ironhold](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/x8tqb3v2). She came hoping a second source would let the trade stop bleeding [Ashvale](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/s07t97v0), so that children born there afterwards might come out whole. [Threnval](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/dxejh6gh) does not know that is part of why she came.
:::
