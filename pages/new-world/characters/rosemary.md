Rosemary runs [[Rosemary and Co.]], [[New Ironhold|New Ironhold's]] general store, and is the only person in [[New Ironhold]] who gives anything away.

- **Pixie**, five inches tall, slight and quick on her feet.
- **No wings.** Stumps, scar tissue, flat where they were.
- **Dim**, the faint under-lit glow pixies have, turned down lower than it ought to be.
- **A voice** barely above nothing. Talking to her means kneeling, every time.
- **An apron** cut down from something made for somebody larger.

She cannot fly, and she has never once asked a customer to reach anything down for her. She will not repeat herself louder, and she is not embarrassed about it. She waits for people to come closer, with more patience than they have.

She is warm without making a show of it. Everyone gets something out of the oven, often a biscuit near the size of her own head, held up until somebody takes it, on the first visit and every visit after, whether or not they buy and whether or not they are pleasant about it. She extends no credit to anyone. She wants to owe nothing to anybody, and to be owed nothing back.

:::secret
She was born property in Cassivere, where that is lawful and The Continental Assembly has no standing to say otherwise, and she was marked for it: for a pixie that means the wings. She does not talk about it, and the few who know the story leave it alone.
:::

:::secret
The "and Co." is not decoration. There are four of them, all dead for years, and she raised every one herself. They are the help.
:::

:::secret
The four were held alongside her in Cassivere, and she brought them over dead in the hold of the ship she came on. She has never taken one of this town's dead. They are the only people who were ever on her side. She will have no living staff. What was done to her was done by people, and something she made cannot wake one morning and decide to do it to her.
:::
