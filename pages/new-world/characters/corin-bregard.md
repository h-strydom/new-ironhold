Guildmaster of [[Quests]] in [[New Ironhold]]. He keeps the board in the [[Pioneers|Pioneers']] tent and speaks for them in town, which makes him the nearest thing New Ironhold has to a government official.

- **Human, in his sixties.** Broad across the shoulders and gone soft through the middle.
- **Grey hair** cropped to the skull, over a weathered face.
- **Ink** stains his fingers to the second knuckle.
- **A ledger** hangs off his belt on a short chain.

He has never handed the ledger to anybody. He writes constantly and does not look up while he does it, and every adventurer off the ship gives him a name before they get anywhere else.

He runs the tent the way a purser keeps a manifest. He speaks for the [[Pioneers]], but he plays no favourites: a notice from [[id:01M854GJFBAES917146Q2016YD|Othamar]] or [[id:01M854JEW07BXK36ETZRPMBTCF|Threnval]] gets the same ink and the same place on the board as one of theirs. Anyone who wastes his time loses their turn to somebody else. Every party that comes back owes him an account of what actually happened out there.

He came over on the third expedition and remembers when the tent was still canvas. He does not talk about why he stopped taking jobs and started handing them out. He reads the gate book every day, and he wants fewer lines in it with no return date against them.
