A goblin band that held ground further west until the gnolls arrived. Losing that fight, they've been pushed east, off their own territory and onto the herb clearing at Bittering Hollow - which is why a band is camped there right now, fouling the stand Threnval wants cleared.

They are the weaker, nearer, more negotiable side of this conflict. They don't want New Ironhold as an enemy - they want somewhere the gnolls aren't, and they're willing to trade labour for it: help building the town, or help working its farms, in exchange for being left alone. That's the whole premise of Friends and Foes, the quest that opens if a party chooses not to wipe them out at Bittering Hollow.

Per Bittering Hollow's existing secret: **crude and improvised weapons only** on this band. Metal-armed goblins are a deliberately later escalation signal and shouldn't be spent here.

**The band's Hobgoblin doubles as its negotiator.** He speaks Common, badly - broken enough to be slow and a little comic, never mocking - and he's the one who opens Friends and Foes if a party tries to talk instead of fight. If a player character speaks Goblin, the broken-Common routine is skipped entirely and the conversation runs normally. Don't create a second, separately-named NPC for this - it's the same Hobgoblin as the warband roster below.

## Creature roster

Named stock 2024 Monster Manual entries, not stat blocks pasted in - confirm the actual numbers against your own book before running this, the same caveat Mill Site's crocodiles carry.

- [Goblin](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16907-goblin) - the base line. Already dual-purpose in the book (melee or a shortbow); an "archer" in this warband is just a Goblin using the bow instead of the scimitar, no separate stat block needed.
- [Hobgoblin](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16925-hobgoblin) - a step up, used sparingly (warband leaders, not rank and file - this is the band's translator, above). An archer variant swaps its melee weapon for a longbow at the same stats.
- [Bugbear](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16817-bugbear) - reserved for the warband's heavy hitter, not a standard encounter. One per fight, if that.
- **Caster variant** - no official goblin/hobgoblin caster block exists in the 2024 MM. Reflavour a low-CR spellcasting NPC (an Acolyte or Apprentice from the Monster Manual's NPC section, whichever your table has to hand) as a goblin shaman. Use rarely - one per set-piece encounter at most, never in a roadside random encounter.

### Warband composition by party size (level 3, per this session's party)

| Party size | Roadside/random encounter | Set-piece (camp, quest climax) |
| - | - | - |
| 4 to 5 | 3 to 4 Goblins, mixed melee/archer | Add 1 Hobgoblin |
| 6 | 4 to 5 Goblins, mixed melee/archer | Add 1 Hobgoblin and, for a hard fight, 1 Bugbear |

The caster variant and a second Hobgoblin are reserved for whatever set-piece a session actually needs (a camp assault gone loud, a betrayal during Friends and Foes) - don't spend them on a random road encounter.
