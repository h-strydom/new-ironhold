Recent arrivals, further west than the goblins and pushing east. A raid drove them out of their previous settlement, and that's the reason they're here at all - not a long-standing claim on this ground, just the nearest place left to go once their last one was taken from them.

They are the more aggressive, better-established threat of the two tribes - better armed and organized than the goblin band they're displacing, and not remotely interested in a deal with New Ironhold. Where the goblins are a diplomacy opportunity, the gnolls are the pressure that creates it: if a party helps the goblins get wiped out instead of settled, the gnolls take the whole strip of ground, and there's no labour-unlock consolation prize on that path.

## Creature roster

Named stock 2024 Monster Manual entries, not stat blocks pasted in - confirm against your own book, same caveat as Mill Site and [Goblins](/a/cmvnyw40908y93hne5ik09b58/moa7t5y7).

- [Gnoll](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16904-gnoll) - the base line and the bulk of any warband. Pack hunters; expect them in groups, not alone.
- [Troll](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/17040-troll) - the tribe's heavy-hitter. One per set-piece encounter, never a standard roadside fight - a troll showing up on a random encounter roll should read as a genuine "something is very wrong" moment, not routine.

**If the party helps clear the goblins out of** [[Bittering Hollow]] **entirely** (the kill branch of [[Bittering Hollow]]), this is the tribe that moves into the vacated ground - the follow-up defend encounter flagged on that page and on Friends and Foes is a gnoll warband from this roster, not a new creature type.
