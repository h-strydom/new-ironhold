Eleven weeks of open water away, and it has almost no say in what happens here.

The mainland is old, settled and thoroughly governed. Every nation on it sits the same assembly, every one of them signed the same treaty, and the calendar [New Ironhold](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/x8tqb3v2) runs on counts from the day they signed it. None of that reaches across the crossing with any force. A question put to the assembly is answered in years. A question put on this continent is answered by whoever is standing there.

That gap is why the expedition works the way it does. The people who funded it cannot supervise it, so what gets decided out here gets decided by the parties on the ground, long before anyone back home could weigh in.

Everything the colony cannot make for itself comes from the mainland, at a round trip of roughly half a year. It is also where every settler came from, and that counts for less with each month they are gone.
