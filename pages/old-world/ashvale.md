A forest, not a city, and as far as anyone on the mainland knows, the only place certain reagents and magical components exist at all. Nobody has managed to transplant, farm or counterfeit what comes out of it.

The scarcity is the whole story. For generations before the treaty, every nation with the reach to try laid claim to Ashvale, because holding it meant holding the supply. None held it outright for long. The forest is hostile to large standing forces, which kept any single claim from sticking without ever stopping the fighting.

Everyone born in or near Ashvale carries something of it. Most of it is small: pigment gone out of the hair, a hand that never formed properly, teeth that came through wrong. Some of it is not small, and a real share of the children marked that badly do not survive their first week.

The mainland has known this for generations and has never treated it as a reason to stop. The reagents have no other source, and the people nearest the forest absorb the cost so that nobody else has to. Nothing to that effect was written into the treaty signed in their forest, and nobody at [[The Continental Assembly]] has raised it since.

The people of its edge are the poorest on the mainland, and everyone who buys from Ashvale knows why. Nobody with a choice lives close enough to work its edge, and the labour is cheap because the people doing it have nowhere else to go.

The people who live there call it [Umbriel](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/am4xsyhf)**'s kiss** and lay it at her door, because hers is the full night when whatever separates the living from the rest goes thin. It is the answer they give aloud. A cursed moon falls on everybody equally and asks nothing of anyone, which is easier to say to a buyer, and easier to live beside, than that the forest is doing it and the trade will not stop for it.

## Why the treaty carries its name

Ashvale is where the treaty was signed, not merely what it was named after. It was the one piece of ground every warring party had a real stake in and none of them controlled, neutral by necessity long before [The Continental Assembly](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/gprg3g2e) made it neutral by law.

## Threnval

[Threnval](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/dxejh6gh)'s trade ran almost entirely on Ashvale reagents before the expedition.
