One of the seven nations that sit the Continental Assembly. An oligarchy: the nobility choose who represents them, and answer to no one for the choice. The most polarising state in Old World politics, and not for its politics but for what it does to the people born into service under it.

Slave mutilation is common there, and treated as ordinary upkeep rather than cruelty. Whatever marks a species as property and costs it the most gets taken: a dragonborn loses a horn, an elf has its ears clipped, a pixie loses its wings.

Rosemary is the one anyone in New Ironhold has actually met. She was born there, came over on an early expedition, and does not discuss it.
