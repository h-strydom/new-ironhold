One of the seven nations that sit the Continental Assembly. A merchant state: no crown, no noble houses, just whichever trading consortium currently controls the most weight. That consortium backs the delegate, and has to buy the backing again next cycle. The seat is for rent, not for life, and Velmora's foreign policy is whatever is currently profitable to the highest bidder.
