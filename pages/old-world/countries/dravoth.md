One of the seven nations that sit the Continental Assembly. Settles its seat the old way: anyone with a real stake in a given vote can challenge for it in ritual combat, and whoever is left standing represents the country on that vote alone. The seat changes hands within a single vote and nowhere else. Nothing about who rules Dravoth day to day works this way, only who speaks for it at the assembly.
