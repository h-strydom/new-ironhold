Its seat is drawn by lot from every citizen, children included, and whoever comes up serves one term, whatever their age, trade or fitness for it. A drawn child sits the assembly in person and casts the vote themselves. Pamora reasons onward from that: a child who can be trusted with a vote can be trusted with work, and children work in Pamora from the age they can be written into a contract. It is a large country of dense working forest. It grew by financial takeover, buying a neighbour's debts and then the neighbour, and each one cost it a seat. It has stopped on purpose.

## Seat

Any child may be drawn, so every household plans around it. A drawn child leaves their trade and their family for the term. The state houses them, names a guardian and a clerk for them in writing, and the family keeps no claim on them until the term ends. Children learn to read before anything else, because a delegate who cannot read a contract cannot be protected by one. Written contracts are sacred in Pamora and spoken ones are not recognised at all. A promise made to a child delegate in a corridor is nothing, so anyone who wants that vote must put the price on paper, and paper is evidence. Most of the work for the consortia is in becoming the guardian.

## Land and trade

Pamora buys raw material, works it inside its own borders and sells the result as luxury goods, which the other countries compete to buy. The working forest supplies the timber and charcoal the workshops run on. Children do a large share of the work, and nobody in Pamora remarks on it. A child's labour is bound by written indenture like any adult's, and the child holds a copy of the paper. That is the country's answer to the charge, and it is enough for Pamora. The paper binds the child as firmly as it binds the master, and as often to the workshop as against it. What it imports and what it sells are not decided yet.

## Ashvale

Pamora buys Ashvale reagents through intermediaries and does not ask what they cost. A country that counts the youngest as full citizens has no standing to be surprised by a forest that marks its children, and it makes no show of being so. Several of Othamar's engineers come from Pamora, and the company knows it is watched.

## What the DM can use

- A bargain with a Pamoran has to exist on paper. Anything agreed aloud holds no weight with them, and they will say so without offence.
- Any Pamoran child may turn out to be the delegate next term. A child met in a workshop is a child who could hold one vote in seven.
- Pamoran engineers at Othamar can read and draft a contract better than most of the party.
- Whoever the party finds guarding a child delegate is the person the consortia have already paid, or the person trying to stop them.
