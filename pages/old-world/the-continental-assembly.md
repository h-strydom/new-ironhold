The Continental Assembly was formalised by the [[Ashvale|Treaty of Ashvale]], signed in year 0 of the calendar. Every country sends one delegate, and each delegate casts a single vote. Among its delegates the assembly is a pure democracy. Inside a country's borders it has no say, and each country governs itself as it chooses.

## Representation

Every country holds one seat whatever its size, so a citizen of a small country carries far more of the continent's voice than a citizen of a large one. That is deliberate: a country that swallows its neighbours loses a seat for every one it absorbs. No country has found a way around this yet, and many have tried.

## How a delegate is chosen

Seven countries sit the assembly.

- **[[Ardessa]]** is a monarchy. The queen takes the seat herself.
- **[[Cassivere]]** is an oligarchy. The nobility choose who represents them, and answer to no one for the choice.
- **[[Dravoth]]** settles each of its votes by ritual combat.
- **[[Velmora]]** is a merchant state. The leading consortium backs the delegate and has to buy that backing again every cycle.
- **[[Ilvaine]]** puts the seat to a vote of its citizens, one vote each, with no council or court standing between them and the seat.
- **Pamora** draws its delegate by lot from every citizen, children included, and a drawn child casts the vote. Its workshops turn imported materials into luxuries the other countries compete to buy.
- **Ondilia** is a theocracy whose temples name the delegate from their own priests. Those temples sell paper, ink and copied books.

## Why it does not reach New Ironhold

Nothing continent-wide moves without every country voting on it, and getting seven governments to agree takes years. Nobody treats that as a flaw. It is why [[New Ironhold]] operates as it does: whatever matters there is settled by expeditions long before the assembly could weigh in.
