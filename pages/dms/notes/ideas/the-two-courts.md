Working notes, deliberately unfinished. The players may never reach either, and\
a lot here is worth leaving open. Nothing is in LegendKeeper.

Tonal reference for the fey and for mythical creatures generally: the Dresden\
Files. Structure and feel only, not names or specifics.

**Solar** is the name for Summer and Spring together, **Umbral** for Winter\
and Autumn. A court is the pair, not the single season.

## The inversion that makes this work

Neither court is the good one, and the reason is sharper than "both have flaws".

**The Summer Court is beautiful, natural, growth-oriented, and has a caste**\
**system.** Only human-like species count as people. Everything else works the\
mines and the farms.

**The Winter Court is brutal, industrial, and genuinely does not care what you**\
**are.** Only whether you are the best.

So a player from a non-human species is treated better in the near-fascist state\
than in the garden. That single fact does more to stop either side reading as\
the nice guys than any amount of grey-shading, and it should be discoverable\
early rather than explained.

## The Solar Court

West-west-south. Summer above, Spring as its minor court.

- **Power is inherited.** Nobody is elected. The ruling houses have always ruled.
- **The rulers are not villains.** They work to improve life for everyone under\
  them, sincerely. They simply never give power up, and cannot see why they\
  would.
- **Only human-like species are treated as people.** Everyone else is labour:\
  mines, farms, whatever needs doing cheaply.
- **Naturalists.** Growth, plants and beasts over metal and industry. They do not\
  mine if they can help it.
- **Magic is what they are best at**, by a distance.

Larger than Winter, and warmer.

## The Umbral Court

East-east-north. Winter above, Autumn as its minor court.

Built on one idea: **the strong decide.** Species does not matter. Birth does not\
matter. Only whether you are the best at the thing.

- The best scientist is whoever is smartest.
- The army is led by whoever is strongest.
- The economy is run by whoever is richest.

* **Industry over magic.** Machines, metal, extraction.
* **Everyone has a decent minimum standard of living.** This is not a place where\
  the poor starve.
* **Rising means being the best or dying in the attempt**, and a great many die\
  in the attempt.

Much smaller than Summer. Partly the climate. Mostly the attrition: selecting\
relentlessly for the best kills an enormous number of people who were merely\
good.

## The moons

The courts track the sky, and the calendar already had the structure waiting.

| Moon | Court | Full |
| - | - | - |
| Solenne | Summer, at its strongest | The 1st of every month |
| Umbriel | Winter, at its strongest | The 33rd of every month |
| Valora | The old sign to go to war | Three or four times a year, never predictable |

Consequences that fall straight out of this and are worth keeping:

**The courts alternate peak power on fixed dates, every single month.** Summer\
opens the month, Winter closes it. Any negotiation, treaty or crossing between\
them happens somewhere on that seesaw, and both sides know exactly where.

**Winter peaks on the night everyone else bolts the door.** Umbriel's Veil is a\
universal ill omen: warded thresholds, no travel, nothing scheduled. It is also\
the night the Umbral Court is strongest. Whether they cause it, benefit from\
it, or are simply blamed for it is worth deciding late rather than early.

**The Sister's Scourge is Valora and Umbriel together.** The war sign and\
Winter's own moon, full on the same night, once a year, on the last week of the\
year. That is the Umbral Court at maximum strength on the night war was\
traditionally declared. Nobody needs to say out loud why the rest of the\
continent shuts its gates.

**Valora "was" the sign to go to war, before the Plains of Dread.** That word is\
doing a lot of work. Read plainly it says the wars stopped, and that the Plains\
are what stopped them. **The courts were the warring clans themselves**, not\
armourers standing behind someone else's fight. The dead they made between them\
are what grew the Plains, and the Plains grew wide enough to cut Summer and\
Winter off from each other outright, which is the real reason nobody in New\
Ironhold has heard of either.

## Why New Ironhold matters, which nobody there knows yet

Summer is west of the wall. Winter is east of it. The direct route between them\
crosses the Plains of Dread, which is not a route.

The only other way is south, around the mountains, **through New Ironhold**.

The settlement is sitting on the only viable trade road between the two great\
powers of the continent, twelve years into not knowing either exists. That is\
the town's long-term destiny and it is worth more than any quest board.

It also reframes the mountain passes completely. A pass west is not a side\
quest. It is the western gate of the southern trade route.

## The companies already map onto the courts

This is almost too neat and should probably be used rather than avoided.

- **Threnval** deals in reagents and old-world magecraft. **Summer** is the\
  magical power.
- **Othamar** hauls in engineers and machinery. **Winter** is the industrial one.

Once contact happens, the two trading companies in New Ironhold become proxies\
for two foreign powers, and probably before anyone realises that is what has\
happened. Players move freely between quest lines by design, so a party can end\
up serving both sides of a rivalry it does not know exists.

## The pass with the nest

The easiest western pass is a nesting ground for something dragon-like or\
wyvern-like. Deliberately **not** a serious threat to a competent party, because\
combat is not the point.

The point is that the party can win, and then has to decide what winning means.\
Getting themselves through is easy. Getting a trade caravan through every month,\
for years, is not the same problem: it means a permanent arrangement, and the\
obvious permanent arrangement is that the nest stops existing.

Three things make this land harder than a standard moral dilemma:

**Make them highly intelligent, and able to negotiate.** The moment the creatures\
can be talked to, "clear the pass" stops being pest control. It also opens a\
third path that is neither detour nor extermination: tribute, escort, a toll, a\
treaty. Whether that holds is the interesting part.

**Make it recurring, not a one-off.** A single caravan is a favour. A caravan\
every month forever is a relationship or a war, and the party has to pick which.

**The party may not be the ones who decide.** If they find the pass and report\
it, Othamar and Threnval will send people who are not asking. The real choice is\
whether to tell anyone what they found, which is a far sharper question than\
whether to swing the sword, and it arrives long after the fight would have.

Worth considering the version where the party clears the pass honourably, agrees\
terms, and watches somebody else break them.

## Open

- Does Winter cause Umbriel's Veil, benefit from it, or merely get blamed?
- What do the non-human species inside Summer actually think, and has anyone\
  ever left for Winter because of it?
- Spring and Autumn are named and otherwise empty. Minor courts are the obvious\
  place to put anyone who does not fit the main two.
