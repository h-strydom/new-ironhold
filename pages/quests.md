Work somebody is willing to pay for, pinned to the board at the Pioneer's tent. Three employers post there: [[Pioneers]], which issues the official surveys, and the two trading companies, [[id:01M854GJFBAES917146Q2016YD|Othamar]] and [[id:01M854JEW07BXK36ETZRPMBTCF|Threnval]], who answer to their own backers and work by the Pioneer's leave.

Anyone can take anything. No job is held for whoever read it first or kept back for those who have been here longest.

## What a job pays

Each notice states one reward for the whole party, and you split it however you like. It is paid in Gilda once the one who posted the job is satisfied. Your own word that you are finished counts for nothing. Report to Guildmaster [Corin Bregard](/a/cmt21jyb000033zod4t3x92rk/cwpwkb2krxdd3nk8rpafsll6) when it is done. He pays every completed job from the ledger, and no company pays you directly.

Some jobs leave more behind in the town than the money does. Nobody will tell you in advance which.

## Before you take one

Read the notice twice. What it does not say is usually the expensive part. Every one was written by somebody with a reason to leave things out, and none of them are paid to keep you alive.
